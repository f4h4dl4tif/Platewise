How to install Platewise
========================

Snap & Cook (photo -> dish -> recipe) uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac:
macOS 27 or later, a Mac with Apple silicon, and Apple Intelligence turned
on. Meal planning, recipes and grocery lists work on macOS 14 or later.
No account or API key is needed.

Platewise isn't notarized by Apple, so on a new Mac macOS blocks it the
first time. Pick ONE of the two ways below, and use the Terminal fixes if
it fails.


OPTION A: Installer package (Platewise-x.x.pkg)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Double-click Platewise-x.x.pkg.
2. If macOS says it "can't be opened" or "could not verify" it: click Done,
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Platewise-x.x.pkg. Enter your password.
3. Follow the installer. Platewise is installed in Applications and opens
   itself.


OPTION B: Disk image (Platewise-x.x.dmg)
----------------------------------------
1. Open the .dmg and drag Platewise onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Platewise from Applications. If macOS blocks it, click Done (don't
   move it to the Bin), then System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   "Open Anyway".


IF IT STILL FAILS: Terminal commands
------------------------------------
Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste a command, press
Return and type your Mac password when asked (nothing shows as you type).

1. The installer package won't open. Install it from Terminal instead
   (change the path if the file isn't in Downloads):

       sudo installer -pkg ~/Downloads/Platewise-*.pkg -target /

2. Platewise is installed but won't open, or macOS says it is "damaged":

       sudo xattr -cr /Applications/Platewise.app && open /Applications/Platewise.app

3. "Open Anyway" doesn't appear, or the .pkg / .dmg itself is blocked:

       xattr -c ~/Downloads/Platewise-*

   then double-click the file again.

4. The Camera button shows a black screen or says access is off:

       tccutil reset Camera local.platewise.Platewise

   then quit and reopen Platewise, click Camera, and choose Allow
   (or turn it on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera).

5. Snap & Cook says Apple Intelligence isn't available. Check it's on in
   System Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri (it may need to finish
   downloading), confirm this shows "arm64" (Apple silicon):

       uname -m

   and that the helper is installed (should print a path):

       ls /Applications/Platewise.app/Contents/MacOS/platewise-ai

   then click Check Again in Platewise's Settings.

6. Platewise keeps asking for your Keychain password, or forgets your
   YouTube key:

       security delete-generic-password -s local.platewise.Platewise

   then reopen Platewise and paste the key again in Settings.

7. Check the macOS version (14+ for Platewise, 27+ for Snap & Cook):

       sw_vers -productVersion


AFTER INSTALLING
----------------
1. Click Settings in the sidebar and check Apple Intelligence says
   "Ready". Dish recognition and recipes run on your Mac, free, and your
   photos never leave it.
2. Optional: paste a free YouTube Data API v3 key to see the top 3
   tutorial videos inside each recipe. Without it you get a YouTube
   search link instead.
3. Snap & Cook: drop a food photo in (or use Camera / Choose / Paste) and
   press Command-Return. Pick the right match to get the full recipe,
   nutrition and videos, then add it to your meal plan or grocery list.
4. Meal Plan: drag recipes from the right onto any day, then press
   Build Grocery List.

Your recipes, plans and photos are saved in
~/Library/Application Support/Platewise. The optional YouTube key is in
your Mac's Keychain.

To uninstall (this also deletes your recipes and plans): quit Platewise,
then run

       rm -rf /Applications/Platewise.app ~/Library/Application\ Support/Platewise
