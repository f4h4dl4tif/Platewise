Platewise is installed

It's in your Applications folder and should already be open.

  • Make sure Apple Intelligence is on (System Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri). Platewise's Settings shows whether it's ready.
  • Optional: add a free YouTube API key to see video picks inside each recipe.
  • Go to Snap & Cook, drop in a food photo, and press ⌘↩.
  • ⌘1–⌘5 switch between Snap & Cook, Meal Plan, Recipes, Grocery List and Settings.
