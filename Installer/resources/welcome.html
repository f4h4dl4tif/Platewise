Platewise

Platewise is a meal planner with Snap & Cook: photograph any dish and Apple Intelligence tells you what it is, writes the full recipe with nutrition, and finds YouTube videos to cook along with.

Plan your week by dragging recipes onto days, and Platewise builds your grocery list, grouped by aisle.

Everything runs on this Mac with Apple's on-device model: no account, no API key, and your photos never leave your Mac.

This installer puts Platewise in your Applications folder and opens it when it's done.

Snap & Cook needs macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon and Apple Intelligence turned on. Meal planning, recipes and grocery lists work on macOS 14 or later.
